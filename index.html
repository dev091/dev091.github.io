Youtube Agency

Youtube Agency is a private production tool used only by its owner to prepare, upload and measure videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (OOFSVILLE, BrickTales and History Atlas). It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos when the owner clicks upload, and to read the owner's own channel statistics (YouTube Analytics) so the next videos can be made better.
