Privacy policy - Youtube Agency

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who uses this app

Youtube Agency is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own YouTube channels. There are no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uses YouTube API Services to: upload videos the owner approved (youtube.upload), read the owner's channel and video details (youtube.readonly), and read the owner's channel statistics such as views, watch time and audience retention (yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to learn which of the owner's videos work, so the next ones can be improved. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account - third-party connections; the owner can also delete the stored tokens from the computer.
